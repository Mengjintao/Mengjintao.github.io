Jintao Meng is a Associate Researcher in Shenzhen Institute of Advanced Technology, CAS . Jintao received his PH.D degree in computer architecture from Institute of Computing Technology in 2016. His research interests include high performance computing, deep learning, bioinformatics, and graph computing. He worked as a Research Engineer from 2008 to 2016 at the Shenzhen Institute of Advanced Technology, CAS. In 2014, he visited the Mathematics and Computing Division (MCS) of Argonne National Laboratory in the United States. In 2016, he served as a visiting scholar at Huawei's Noah's Ark Laboratory (Hong Kong). From 2017 to 2020, he is a senior Research Engineer at Tencent AI Lab. Since 2020, he served as an associate researcher at the High Performance Computing Center of Shenzhen Institute of Advanced Technology, CAS.
