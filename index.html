Jintao Meng is an Associate Researcher at the Shenzhen Institute of Advanced Technology, CAS. He received his Ph.D. in computer architecture from the Institute of Computing Technology in 2016. His research interests include high-performance computing, deep learning, bioinformatics, and graph computing.

He has held positions at Tencent AI Lab (Senior Research Engineer), Huawei's Noah's Ark Laboratory (Visiting Scholar), and Argonne National Laboratory (Visiting Scholar).
